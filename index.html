About Us

Making is Learning

Teacher Studio brings together educators from classrooms, museums, libraries, and beyond to explore maker-based pedagogy through hands-on experiences.

Each month, we gather—in person at hub locations or virtually via Zoom—to create something together, share strategies, and build a supportive community of practice.
