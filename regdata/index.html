    Method & caveats

    How these numbers were built

    • People. 648 form rows were merged into 316 people by matching email, then name, then fixing a few typos by hand. When someone's details changed, their most recent answer was kept.
    • Workshop dates and topics come from the Resource Archive on teacherstudio.org. Each registration counts toward the next workshop on or after its timestamp. Some archive dates don't match the registrations: Apr 2023 (listed as a Monday), Nov 2024, Nov 2025 and May 2026 (listed as a Friday). In those months, people kept registering after the listed date, and nine of them registered again for the following month. Those workshops were moved to the third Thursday.
    • Topics are missing for Sep 2023, May 2024, Jan 2025, Sep 2025, Feb 2026 and Apr 2026. "Puppets – 3 ways!" is archived as July 16, 2026. Its description mentions Anwar Floyd-Pruitt, who died in 2025, so it was probably the Feb or Apr 2026 session.
    • Role, organization type and location were assigned by keyword rules plus hand fixes. 17 people couldn't be placed geographically. Treat these groupings as close estimates, not exact counts.
    • Registrations are not attendance. The form doesn't record who showed up. Oct 2022 (Cardboard Mask Making) came before this form was in use.
    • Small samples. Each month has only 2–4 data points, so the month and theme patterns are directional.
